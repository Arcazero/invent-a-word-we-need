**noddrift** (n.) — the slow drift out of a conversation while your head keeps nodding politely.

An original coinage for make.fun's "Invent a Word We Need" bounty ($5 in USDC). `noddrift` names the moment your attention quietly leaves a conversation while your head carries on nodding as if you were still in it — a small, universal social experience English has no single word for. The entry program writes the word and its one-line definition (92 characters) to `entry.txt` in the output directory, exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
